# Include useful figures in paper wiki entries

- **Feedback:** Include captioned overview or comparison figures when they make a paper easier to understand, with at most three figures stored beside the wiki page.
- **Affected workflow:** The [ingest-paper skill](../.github/skills/ingest-paper/SKILL.md#ingest-a-selected-paper) defines selection, filenames, source attribution, and review.
- **Expected improvement:** Readers can see important setups or comparisons in context without adding unnecessary images to the five-minute core.
