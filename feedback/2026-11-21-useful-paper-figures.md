# Include useful figures in paper wiki entries

- **Feedback:** Prefer cropping a clear, relevant overview figure from the paper and including its caption. If no suitable figure can be included, make an original overview diagram and verify every visual claim against the source. Keep at most three figures beside the wiki page.
- **Affected workflow:** The [ingest-paper skill](../.github/skills/ingest-paper/SKILL.md#ingest-a-selected-paper) defines selection, filenames, source attribution, and review.
- **Expected improvement:** Readers can see how the authors present the setup or comparison. Original diagrams clarify the paper without introducing unsupported details or adding unnecessary images to the five-minute core.
