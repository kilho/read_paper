# Best Paper award resources

Use the indexes to discover winners across venues and years. Verify each award against the organizer's announcement for the matching edition before using it as a ranking signal. This list is a starting point, not comprehensive coverage. Sources checked 2026-11-21.

## Cross-venue and historical indexes

| Resource | Coverage and limits |
|---|---|
| [FeijiangHan/Top-Conference-Best-Papers](https://github.com/FeijiangHan/Top-Conference-Best-Papers) | Community-maintained GitHub list focused on recent AI, ML, NLP, and vision conferences. Year coverage varies by venue. |
| [Jeff Huang: Best Paper Awards in Computer Science](https://jeffhuang.com/best_paper_awards/) | Historical index covering 32 computing conferences starting in 1996, including SIGIR, CIKM, WWW, ACL, CVPR, and ICCV. Check newer editions separately. |
| [ACL Wiki: Best paper awards](https://www.aclweb.org/aclwiki/Best_paper_awards) | Historical NLP award lists. Use the individual conference announcements below for edition-specific confirmation. |
| [CS-Pedia: WSDM Best Papers](https://cs-pedia.io/best-papers/wsdm) | A secondary index for selected WSDM years, not a complete record. |

Record the **exact award label**, venue, year, and official evidence URL. Best Paper, Outstanding Paper, Best Student Paper, Honorable Mention, and Test of Time are different distinctions. A nomination, oral presentation, or highlight is not a Best Paper win. If the award cannot be verified, leave it unknown.
